# GDG on Campus Dr. B. R. Ambedkar National Institute of Technology - Jalandhar, India

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of **Google Developer Groups on Campus, NIT Jalandhar (GDGC NITJ)**.

We are a **student-led community** at NIT Jalandhar that brings together developers, designers, and innovators to **learn, build, and grow** with the latest technologies. Our mission is to create a culture of collaboration, technical excellence, and innovation within the campus, empowering students to become future-ready professionals.

---

## About Us

GDG on Campus NIT Jalandhar provides a platform where students can:

* Learn by organizing and attending **hands-on workshops, hackathons, and study jams**
* Build impactful **projects and products** with real-world applications
* Collaborate with peers across different technical domains
* Grow by sharing knowledge and fostering an **inclusive, developer-first community**

---

## Teams at GDGC NITJ

We currently operate through **seven specialized teams**, each focusing on a domain of modern technology:

* **AI/ML** : Research and projects in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
* **App Development** : Building scalable Android/iOS applications
* **Competitive Programming (CP)** : Problem-solving, algorithms, and data structures for coding excellence
* **Cyber Security** : Exploring security practices, ethical hacking, and digital safety
* **DevSecOps** : Infrastructure, automation, and deployment best practices
* **Web Development** : Designing and developing modern web solutions
* **Women in Tech** : Driving diversity and inclusion in technology
